DrawMe Privacy Policy

Quick Summary for Parents and Reviewers

DrawMe is an offline-first coloring and drawing application for young children. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. All artworks, coloring progress, and profiles remain strictly on your Android device in private local storage.

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to DrawMe ("the App"), developed and published by NTFSApps ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children, parents, and caregivers who use our software.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and use the DrawMe application (Android package: com.ntfsapps.drawme). Because DrawMe is created specifically for young children, we adhere strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many mobile apps, DrawMe is built with a zero-collection architecture:

  • No Names or Contact Information: We do not require or collect real names, postal addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or social media handles.
  • No Location Data: We never request, access, or track GPS, cellular, or Wi-Fi location coordinates (neither precise nor coarse location).
  • No Persistent Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, IMEI, MAC address, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Biometric or Sensory Data: We do not access the device camera, microphone, face recognition, or voice inputs.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: The application does not collect payment details, credit card numbers, or banking credentials.
  • No User Account or Login Credentials: No registration, account creation, or online authentication is ever required to play or draw.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

DrawMe functions 100% offline. To deliver a seamless drawing and coloring experience, the application stores limited functional data exclusively inside the device's sandboxed, private app directory (Context.getFilesDir()) and an internal private SQLite database:

  • Child Profiles: A local display nickname or label entered by the child or parent, an illustrative avatar choice, and a selected age group bracket (3–5, 6–8, or 9+). This information is solely used to customize brush sizing and organize drawings.
  • Saved Artwork & Drawings: The visual drawings, colors, and line art filled by the child.
  • Recents & Favorites: Locally indexed lists of recently colored sketches or bookmarked templates.
🔒

Isolated App Sandbox

All local files are stored within Android's private internal storage sandbox. No other applications on your device have permission to read these files, and they are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.

4. Device Permissions Policy

DrawMe requests zero dangerous or runtime privacy permissions.

Permission Group Status Rationale & Safeguard

Location (GPS / Network) Not Used No geolocation tracking or geofencing.
Contacts & Accounts Not Used No phonebook or social account access.
Broad External Storage (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Not Used We only use app-private storage. No shared file access required.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

In strict compliance with Google Play Families Policy guidelines:

  • Zero Commercial Ads: DrawMe contains no advertisements. There are no banners, interstitials, rewarded videos, or third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, or IronSource).
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry SDKs: We do not bundle third-party tracking frameworks (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or Mixpanel).
  • Zero Social Media Plugins: There are no embedded Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, or Twitter SDKs.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because our application is intended for young children, we prioritize the highest standard of child safety:

  • We do not condition a child's participation in any activity on the disclosure of more personal information than is reasonably necessary. In fact, no personal information is required or collected at all.
  • We do not employ interest-based advertising or behavioral profiling.
  • Children cannot communicate with other users, share content publicly online, or post artwork to external web platforms from within the app.

7. Parental Rights, Controls & Data Deletion

Parents and legal guardians have absolute ownership and control over all content generated within DrawMe:

  • Parental Gate: Administrative operations (such as deleting a child profile or clearing artwork galleries) are protected by a mathematical parental gate to prevent accidental actions by young children.
  • Instant Deletion: Deleting a child's profile from the app immediately and permanently erases all associated drawings and local profile metadata from the device.
  • Full Device Data Wipe: Parents can instantly remove 100% of the application's data at any moment via Android Settings:
    Settings → Apps → DrawMe → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the App: Because all data resides in private app storage, uninstalling DrawMe from your Android device permanently destroys all local drawings and profile records.

8. Optional System Printing

DrawMe includes an optional physical printing feature allowing parents to print blank coloring templates or completed drawings to a home printer. This utilizes Android's official system PrintManager API. The print job is handled directly between your Android OS and your local printer driver; no data is ever routed through external NTFSApps servers.

9. Changes & Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or evolving regulatory standards. Any modifications will be posted directly to this public URL with an updated "Effective Date". If any material change were ever made regarding child privacy, we would notify parents through prominent notices in the app update notes on Google Play.

Caregiver Guide

Parental Controls & Math Gate Guide

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Audience: Parents & Teachers 🔒 Protection: Adult Challenge Gate

We believe parents should always be in full command of their child's digital creative space. DrawMe incorporates thoughtful safeguards to keep toddlers and children focused on drawing while preventing unintended deletions or configuration changes.

1. How the Math Parental Gate Works

Whenever a sensitive action is initiated—such as deleting an artist profile, resetting drawing history, or entering parent settings—DrawMe presents a randomly generated mathematical challenge (for example: "Solve: 14 + 23").

  • Young children (ages 3 to 8) cannot easily bypass this barrier, protecting their artwork from accidental loss.
  • No internet connection or account login is required to solve the challenge.
  • No sensitive passwords or PINs are stored or transmitted.

2. Managing Child Profiles

Families with multiple siblings can create individual local profiles directly from the home screen:

  • Profile Customization: Choose a friendly nickname, pick a colorful animal or character avatar, and select an age tier (3–5, 6–8, or 9+).
  • Age-Adapted Brushes: Younger children (3–5) receive larger, more forgiving touch targets and simplified palette tools; older children enjoy precision brush sizing.
  • Isolated Galleries: Each child's saved masterpieces are organized neatly under their own profile.

3. Printing for Real-World Play

To bring the fun into the real world, parents can tap the Print button on any sketch to produce physical coloring sheets using regular paper and crayons. Printing uses your device's native system print service without transmitting artwork to external web servers.
